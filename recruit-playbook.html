How to Use This Toolkit

This toolkit covers advisor recruiting — advisors who aren't with your firm yet and are weighing a move. Every candidate is in one of three situations, each with its own section and ready-to-use materials.

🌐

Inbound

They found you. Website visitors, readers, event registrants. Give them a first step that needs no call.

👋

Outreach

💬

In Talks

You're talking. Meetings, visits, offers. Answer their three questions — help them decide.

Your Walkthrough Session

This toolkit is designed to be walked through together. Your Knomee customer success contact will schedule a 45-minute working session to:

  • Decide where to start — Inbound, Outreach, or both
  • Put your Go Independent link on your website
  • Customize the outreach samples and set up invite links for each recruiter
  • Walk through My Candidates and a candidate profile together
  • Agree on success metrics

You'll leave with your website invite live and your first outreach ready to send.

Ground Rules for Recruiting

Advisors exploring a move are taking a real risk by talking to you. Every play in this toolkit follows four rules:

  • Personal channels only. Never email or message a candidate at their current firm — those channels are monitored.
  • Never ask about their book. No client names, lists, or account details. The flow is about the advisor, not their clients.
  • Let them set the pace. Deferred comp, bonus cycles, and family life set the timeline — not your quarter.
  • Compliance first. Run every template past your compliance team before it goes out.

What Your Candidate Experiences

Knomee has a dedicated flow for advisors weighing a move: the same five-adventure shape your advisors' clients walk through, pointed at a different decision. It takes about 8 minutes, on their own, before they talk to anyone.

Practice Joy

What they want the practice to give them — equity, control, their team's future, income — and the moment that reminds them why they do this.

2

What's on their mind about going independent — the concerns that keep them up, and the hopes they daydream about.

4

The practice they're building toward — where, with whom, how far out — and a postcard from Future You.

5

The move they're considering, when, why, who else has a say, and the one thing that would have to be settled first.

What Your Team Gets

Every completion lands in My Candidates, your candidates dashboard, with its candidate profile behind it:

  • Advisor Readiness — a 0–100 Recruitment Quotient (RQ) with Intent, Clarity, and Receptivity behind it, a tier, how quickly they'll move, and their motivators and apprehensions

Firm-Neutral by Design

Their three questions are written for every firm they're considering — including yours. That's what makes the flow credible to an advisor who's marketed to constantly. It also means your answers to those three questions are the real pitch: have them ready, in writing.

One Call to Action

Every first touch uses the same call to action — the 8-minute Knomee flow — never “schedule a confidential call.” The call still happens. It's just a better one, because you already know what they're aiming for.

Two Jobs in One

How to Read This One

High on all three, with one blocker left: this candidate is Ready, blocked on one thing. Their Top Action names it — whether their clients come with them.

Don't open anywhere else. Answer it with evidence in the first ten minutes: retention for teams like theirs, including the moves that went worst. Reassurance won't do it.

An illustrative example. Every candidate profile carries this Advisor Readiness card, with the answers behind each score one tap away.

The Three Dimensions

🎯

Intent

Is this a live decision, or a recurring mood?

  • HighA live decision. Get specific: timeline, transition plan, the offer.
  • LowA recurring mood. Shorten the perceived transition — one date to react to, not a pitch.
🔭

Clarity

Do they know what kind of independence they want?

  • HighMatch their picture to your platform, point by point.
  • LowA diagnostic conversation, not a platform conversation. Find the actual complaint first.
🤝

Receptivity

Would they let a platform help?

  • HighBring in your transition team early. They want the help.
  • LowThey'd rather do it alone. Lead with peers, and with what they'd keep control of.

Motivators, Velocity, and the Second Seat

  • Motivators come from Practice Joy — what they want the practice to give them. Open on those. If income isn't on the list, don't lead with payout.
  • How Quickly Will This Advisor Move? High velocity: keep up, on a two-week cadence with the transition plan ready. Lower: a dated next step, then the six-month check-in.
  • Who else has a say — a team, a business partner, a spouse — comes from The Move. Get them in the room early.

Open the Recruiting Playbook Tab Before Every Call

Every candidate profile has one. It holds the Top Action, Conversation Starters built from their own words, the Questions They May Ask — their three, with how to answer each — and Words to Use and Words to Avoid. The Visit Prep sample in In Talks turns it into a one-page plan.

Optimize

Test Before You Launch

Don't guess which message will land — test it. Tag your Go Independent link so you can see which channel and message drive completions (not just clicks). Personal invites need no tags: each one is already tied to the person you sent it to.

1
Copy your Go Independent link.
2
Tag it with UTM parameters. knomeeurl.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=go_independent&utm_content=post1
3
Compare completions by source and message — then adapt.

Small Lists, Small Batches

Recruiting lists are short, so test in batches, not percentages. Alternate Option 1 and Option 2 across your next 20 outreach messages, compare replies and how many reach Completed in My Candidates, then keep the winner. The Outreach Option 1 / Option 2 samples are built to be your first test.
